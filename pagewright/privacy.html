Pagewright

Privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

The short version

Pagewright does not collect, store, or share any information about you or your files. Everything happens on your own PC.

Who we are

Pagewright is published by ArcaneThunder.

Your files

The PDFs and images you open in Pagewright are processed locally on your device. They are never uploaded, and Pagewright sends nothing about them anywhere.

Personal information and analytics

Pagewright has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. It does not collect usage data, crash reports, or device identifiers.

Purchases

Pagewright Pro is an optional add-on sold through the Microsoft Store. To check whether it's on sale, show its price, take a purchase, and check whether you own it, Pagewright uses the Microsoft Store service built into Windows, which talks to Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. That is the only network use in Pagewright. Pagewright never sees your payment details or your Microsoft account.

Children

Pagewright collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the publisher by opening an issue at github.com/AaronOptimus/AaronOptimus.github.io/issues (a free GitHub account is needed), or through the Pagewright listing in the Microsoft Store. See also Pagewright support.
